Why custom art sells the story

Stock images and templates are made for everyone — which means they're made for no one. Custom art starts from your story.

  • Reads as your genre at thumbnail size — Most readers meet your book small, in a storefront grid. A cover built for that size tells the right readers "this is for you" at a glance.
  • One focal point, nothing competing — Clear type, one strong image, and room for the title — so nothing about the design quietly works against the book.
  • Your characters, not stock faces — Character art drawn from your descriptions gives your series a face readers recognize across books and posts.
  • Made to be shared — The same art works as promo: posts, your author site, and your newsletter, all in one matching look.

Made for you, not pulled from a template library. Every piece starts with your story, your characters, and your world — drawn by hand in Procreate, start to finish. No AI.

What makes a cover work?

Same illustration, two very different covers. One is a demonstration that uses design choices which quietly work against a book; the other is a finished cover from my portfolio.

What makes the difference: clear type that reads at a glance, one focal point instead of clutter, color and contrast that keep everything readable, artwork composed with room for the title, and styling that tells the right readers this is their kind of story.

The thumbnail test. Most readers meet your book at this size, in a storefront grid. A clear title and one strong image remain legible — a cluttered cover turns into noise. That's why a cover should be designed to hold up at this size, not just full-size.

A strong cover is one part of a book's success — the story, blurb, reviews, and promotion all matter too. What good design does is make sure the right readers stop, understand your genre at a glance, and trust that the book inside is made with care.

Questions, answered

The things authors ask before they book — so you can decide with everything on the table.

How much does a custom cover or character piece cost?

Character art starts at $25 for a bust (half-body from $40, full-body from $60, shaded full-body pieces up to $100). Book covers start at $150, and the premium package — character art plus a full cover — starts at $350. Your exact quote depends on detail, the number of characters, and the background. You'll always get your confirmed quote before you book.

What's included in the price?

Every commission includes a sketch you approve before final art begins, 2 revision rounds, and high-resolution final files. Covers also include title and author name typesetting, sizing for ebook or paperback, and print-ready files.

How do I know which service is right for my book?

If your book needs a face in the store, start with a cover. If you want readers to meet your characters — for promos, your site, or social posts — start with character art. The premium package pairs both with a matching look. Not sure? DM me and I'll point you to the right one — no pressure to book.

What do you need from me to get started?

Just your idea and any references you have: character descriptions, your genre, and scenes or moments you love. For a cover, it also helps to know where the book will be published (ebook, paperback, or both) so the files are sized correctly from the start.

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit books your slot. You'll see and approve the finished piece first; the balance is then due before your final files are delivered.

How many revisions do I get?

Two revision rounds are included with every piece, on top of approving the sketch before final art begins. If you need more changes after that, extra rounds are available for a per-round fee — we'll agree on it before any extra work starts.

Do you use AI in your art?

No. Every piece is drawn by hand in Procreate, start to finish — sketch, revisions, and final art. Commissioning me means commissioning a person.

How long will my piece take?

It varies from project to project — a single bust and a full cover with character art are very different amounts of work. When you DM me, I'll give you a timeframe for your specific piece before you book, so you know what to expect.

What usage rights do I receive?

Tell me how you plan to use the piece when you message me — whether it's for personal use or for a published book and its promotion — and we'll confirm exactly what's included for your project before you book, so there are no surprises later.
